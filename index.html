What I Do

Services & Expertise

Genesys Cloud Architecture & Implementation

End-to-end design and deployment of Genesys Cloud CX platforms — from greenfield builds and complex migrations to IVR optimisation, routing strategy, and ACD configuration. Delivered for enterprise-scale contact centres across multiple industries.

AI & LLM Integration

Production-grade AI voice bots, intelligent chatbots, and RAG pipelines that integrate seamlessly with CCaaS platforms. Experience building with Claude API, ElevenLabs TTS, and Vertex AI — from architecture through to deployment and monitoring.

Google Cloud Platform (GCP) Solutions

Designing and deploying cloud-native architectures on GCP — including CCAI (Dialogflow CX, Agent Assist), BigQuery analytics pipelines, Pub/Sub event streaming, and Cloud Run deployments for contact centre workloads.

Workforce Management (WFM) Consulting

Strategic WFM advisory covering forecasting models, scheduling optimisation, and adherence frameworks. Hands-on experience configuring Genesys WFM and integrating third-party WFM platforms to reduce overstaffing and improve service levels.

CCaaS Automation & n8n Workflow Design

Building scalable automation workflows with n8n — connecting CRM systems, ticketing platforms, telephony APIs, and AI services into cohesive, event-driven pipelines. From lead enrichment and sales prospecting to post-call follow-up automation.

Contact Centre Analytics & Speech Mining

Unlocking insight from voice data using platforms like Genesys SpeechMiner and interaction analytics tooling. Design of custom dashboards, agent performance frameworks, and quality scoring models that drive measurable CX improvement.

About

The Architect Behind the Work

I'm Stephan Taljaard, a Contact Centre & AI Solutions Architect based in Brno, Czech Republic. With over a decade of experience in enterprise CCaaS environments, I specialise in the intersection of intelligent automation, cloud architecture, and human-centred contact centre design.

My work spans Genesys Cloud platform engineering, production AI deployments using large language models and voice synthesis, and cloud-native architectures on Google Cloud Platform. I've delivered projects for clients across financial services, telecommunications, retail, and utilities — from 50-seat SME contact centres to 1,500-seat enterprise deployments.

I'm particularly focused on practical AI integration: not AI for its own sake, but AI that measurably improves customer experience, agent productivity, and operational efficiency. I bridge the gap between cutting-edge language model capabilities and the real-world constraints of contact centre operations.

When I'm not architecting platforms, I'm building automation workflows, contributing to the n8n community, and exploring what the next generation of voice AI will enable for enterprise customer service. What genuinely excites me about this field is that it never stands still — and neither does the impact. There's nothing quite like watching a customer's operation transform and knowing your work made that possible. Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with my wife, hiking, and gaming.
