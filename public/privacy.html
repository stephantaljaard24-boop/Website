Who is responsible

Stephan Taljaard, Brno, Czech Republic, operates this website and is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: stephantaljaard24@gmail.com.

The Digital Twin AI chat

The chat on this site is an AI assistant that answers questions about my professional background. Its replies are generated by an AI model, may be inaccurate or incomplete, and are not professional advice. It is not a person; if something matters, please email me or message me on LinkedIn.

  • What is processed: the messages you type and the earlier messages from your current chat, so the assistant has context.
  • Where it goes: your messages are sent to my server (hosted on Cloudflare) and forwarded to OpenRouter, which passes them to an AI model provider to generate the reply. These providers process the data under their own privacy policies.
  • What I keep: the conversation exists in your browser while the page is open and is gone when you close or reload it. My server does not save the content of your messages. Cloudflare may keep standard technical request logs (such as IP address and timestamp) for delivery, security and troubleshooting.
  • Abuse protection: a short-lived counter tied to your IP address (about two minutes) limits how many messages can be sent in a short time.
  • Please do not enter sensitive information (such as health data, passwords or financial details) or confidential client information into the chat.

Contacting me by email, LinkedIn or WhatsApp

If you email me, message me on LinkedIn, or open a WhatsApp chat with me, I receive the details you provide (for example your name, contact details and message) and use them only to reply to your enquiry and to follow up on a possible engagement. I keep this correspondence for as long as needed for that purpose and then delete it, unless a business or legal reason requires me to keep it longer. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • WhatsApp (Meta)
  • LinkedIn

Cookies, analytics and fonts

This website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and does not store data in your browser. It does load its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers and your IP address is transmitted to Google when the page loads.

Why I process data (legal basis)

I process the data above on the basis of my legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in running this website, answering enquiries and keeping the chat secure, and, where you contact me about a possible engagement, to take steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
